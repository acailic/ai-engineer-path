# Sources

Everything in this repository is synthesized from the open course material listed here. Every chapter names the lectures, readings and book chapters it draws on. If a chapter and its source disagree, the source wins: the lectures and the book are the authority, this repository is the study guide.

## How the material was used

- Lecture videos were watched through machine transcripts made for this project. The transcripts are not published here; the videos are the source and every citation links to the exact moment.
- Slide decks, readings and the CMU book were read as reference. Nothing is copied: all explanations are rewritten, and short quotations are marked and attributed.
- The CMU book is licensed CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; this repository paraphrases and links to it.
- Lectures were recorded between September 2024 and June 2026. Fast-moving details (model names, scores, products, policies) are true as of the lecture date unless a chapter says otherwise.
