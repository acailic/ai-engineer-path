# Lecture guides

One guide per lecture of the four courses: 7 guides. A guide maps the whole lecture from its first minute to its last, in parts of a few minutes, and says for each part where this book teaches the same thing. Where no chapter teaches it, the guide itself says what the lecturer teaches there; the column "Guide only" counts those parts. Use a guide before or after watching a lecture, or instead of it when you want the content of a lecture that no chapter covers in depth.

Every part heading links to the moment in the video where the part begins.
